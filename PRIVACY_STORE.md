# Tavo — Privacy Policy (Microsoft Store edition)

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

**Short version:** Tavo has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
Everything it remembers stays on your computer, and Tavo never sends your
personal information or what you watch to the developer.

## What Tavo stores (only on your PC)
Tavo keeps a few files in its own app storage on your computer:
- your settings, your library folders, where you stopped each video, and the sound-sync setting for each video;
- thumbnails Tavo makes of your videos;
- wallpaper pictures you choose;
- your Live TV choices: your playlist file or link, which free public channel lists you turned on, and copies of those lists;
- your recent stream links;
- a small error log (file names and error messages, never full web links with access tokens), kept under 512 KB, to help fix problems.

None of this leaves your PC. Uninstalling Tavo removes it.

## When Tavo goes online
Only when you use a feature that needs the internet:

| What you do | Who Tavo talks to |
|---|---|
| Turn on free public channels in Live TV | iptv-org (iptv-org.github.io) for the channel lists, then each channel's own server to check it's on air and to play it |
| Load your own playlist link | the website in that link, then each channel's server |
| Open Radio | radio-browser.info for the station list, then the station's server |
| Play a stream link | the server in that link |
| Random wallpaper or a picture link | picsum.photos, or the link you gave |

Those servers see your IP address, like any website you visit. Tavo sends them
only what's needed to fetch the list or stream you asked for.

## Children
Tavo doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
